TRON is home to the largest share of global USDT activity. USDT TRC-20 is the most widely used stablecoin rail in crypto — fast, cheap, and deeply liquid.

But moving value out of TRON has historically been painful:

❌ Custodial risk	Funds held by a third party during transfer

TRON bridges solve this by connecting TRON liquidity to the wider multi-chain economy — without accounts, without identity checks, and without custody loss.
